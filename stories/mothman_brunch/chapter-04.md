# Chapter Four: The Hospital

NOT FINISHED - DON'T READ

Julie was recovering much quicker than Steve. Some locals had heard their screaming that night on the beach and rushed over to find them both unconscious. From there, they were whisked away to Nanaimo Regional General Hospital. Since they were a couple and their symptoms we the same they managed to get a room together. Now, a couple of days later, Julie was able to get up and walk around. Steve was slowly getting better, but he still got a bit lightheaded if he wasn't laying down. In spite of that, the doctors were happy with his progression, although they still weren't sure what would have cause both Julie and Steve to pass out like that.

It was mid-morning when she got up to go get them both coffees from the hospital cafe. It all seemed so crazy to her. As far as she could remember, beyond medical procedures, she had never actually lost consciousness at any point in her life. Additionally, she had no memory of what happened that night on the beach. All she had were vague sensations and feelings. There was a warm, all encompassing, red light, a feeling of terror, followed by calm, and the next thing she knew, she was waking up in the hospital.

Since they were stuck up there, she had to call into work to let them know she would be off for a few days. The dynamics in her office were pretty toxic and she dreaded ever calling in sick. Her boss was so horrible about it. Something was different this time though. As soon as the thought of work popped into her head, she was able to calmly pick up the phone and call in. No pacing around needed. No deep breaths before.

"Hey, Bob. How's it going?"

"Julie! Where the heck are you? It's 10:15. Things aren't getting done."

Casually, as if she didn't hear him, Julie said "Oh nice, ya. Anyways, I won't be able to come in today. Not feeling well. I'll touch base with you tomorrow. Toodles."

Bob, flabbergasted, started a speech that he never got to finish. "Julie. No, this isn't acceptable. I need you here by..."

*CLICK*

Julie hung up and silenced her phone. She wouldn't realize until next week, when she was back in the office, that she no longer had any feelings, good or bad, about her boss. It was actually her co-workers who pointed it out. She didn't totally know what to make of that, but she knew she was happy. Eventually Bob got the message that she could no longer be pushed around.

As she walked back to their room with the coffees, she couldn't help but reflect on the last time they were in a medical setting. It was another screening for the dry patch on Steve's hand. Although she was no longer nervous about work, she still couldn't relax about the diagnosis. Cancer, but containable for now. She meant to get a second opinion while in the hospital, just in case, but she needed a way to do it secretly, maybe while Steve was asleep.

Rounding the corner to the hall with their room, she was surprised to see the door closed. Her brow furrowed when she looked through the glass of the door to see two men, dressed in black suits, sitting and talking with Steve.

She didn't mean to be so dramatic, but she did have to kick open the door, as it was heavy and her hands were full with the coffees.
