# Mothman Brunch

I had the idea for this one when I got interested in Mothman lore. Mothman is a man sized moth creature with glowing red eyes. There was a hollywood movie (that I haven't seen) called *Mothman Prophecies*, but I think there was almost no monster screen time. 

Anyways, my silly idea for this one was to blend a spooky cryptid story with elements of Hallmark movie or "beach read" tropes:
- Main character moves back to town
- They rekindle an old relationship
- (In the books) lots of gratuitous descriptions of food and drink

**Note:** As of now, what I've got for this story is still sitting in my notebook.
