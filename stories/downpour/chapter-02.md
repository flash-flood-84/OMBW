Peter had to push the door in slightly to lock it. It never sat in the frame properly after the flood from the massage parlour on the floor above. The deadbolt made a reassuring clang and thud, but, out of habit, Peter still tried opening the door a few times.

He patted his pockets.

Wallet
Keys
Notepad

He rested his hand on his hip, considering the empty space there. *No. Guns just make trouble. Umbrella should be enough.* He popped another mint and headed down the dim hallway to the stairwell. On his way down to the street level he bitterly thought of his old plans to get out of this dump and into a proper office. One in a building with a working elevator and cleaners. It would have all been possible if he had just kept his streak of high profile cases going.

The puddle by the street exit door didn't bode well. Stepping out onto the street, he was hit by a wind he did not expect. Swivelling to face the gust, he gripped his umbrella tightly and opened it. His side had only been half soaked by the rain before he was safely under cover.

*Could have been worse,* he shrugged.

The streetlights seemed to burn brighter through the rain now that the sun had set. He checked his watch. 7:00pm. *I could make it to the docks by 7:30 if I huff it.*
