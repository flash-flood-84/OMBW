# Downpour

I originally started this story as an improvised bedtime story for my kids. It was called princess detective. They liked it so much that I thought I could continue the story and write it. Well I got a few pages in and my eldest daughter said she didn't like it. So then I decided to write it for myself. It would just be basic private investigator noir - something I've always admired, but the twist I'm putting on it is that it is very rainy. Groundbreaking, I know, but it was enough to get me excited about writing the story.
