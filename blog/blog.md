# Late October 2026

The beginning!

Earlier this year I had wanted to get another creative activity that was screenless. Also, ever since finishing my BA in English I had wanted to be a writer. I never really got up the courage and also I ended up getting into IT as a good, stable career, so it fell off my radar.

But now things felt different and also I had a newfound sense of fearlessness when it came to making bad art. Because one of my goals was simply to occupy my time without a screen, I kind of didn't care about the output. But the secret is that this is the only way to really start writing - at least for me.

So about six months later I now have 3 or 4 beginnings of stories and I'm ready to put them out into the world. Of course, that goes back on my screenless goal, but now really I am more excited about the writing than anything else and trying to contain and track my stories in a notebook has become painful. 

And so begins the adventure. My current goal is to just one of these initial stories finished. If I can do that then I will be proud - even if it sucks.
